# The Number Chooses the Route

## Brief

- **Audience:** general viewers curious about what happens after a bag disappears behind the terminal wall.
- **Form:** factual mechanism explainer using a deliberately simplified, fictional terminal.
- **Duration:** exactly 24.000 seconds, frames `[0,720)` at 30 fps. All frame intervals are zero-based with exclusive ends.
- **Voice:** one original adult narrator, clear General American English, steady mid-register, conversational urgency. Actual performer/provider and voice identifier are unselected.
- **Picture:** 1080 × 1920, full 3D miniature terminal cutaway; higher plan-to-section caption profile.
- **Mechanism:** a scanned bag identity is linked to flight data; position tracking lets the controller operate a physical diverter for the correct bag.
- **Resolution:** the blue suitcase arrives at the collection belt allocated to fictional **FLIGHT 24**, ready for loading staff. A collection belt is not an aircraft boarding gate or a baggage-claim carousel.

>
> Our bag arrives, and a timed diverter pushes it onto a different belt.
>
> That belt leads to Flight Twenty Four's collection point, ready for the loading crew. The belt moves it. Its identity chooses the route.

## Causal story

| Role | Story action | What the audience learns |
|---|---|---|
| Familiar setup and limit | A suitcase moves behind the terminal wall; two possible destinations are revealed. | Transport alone does not supply a routing decision. |
| First mechanism | A tag reader attempts identification. | The bag has an identity independent of its appearance. |
| Failure and recovery | An unreadable barcode causes a brief hold; an operator checks the captured tag image and confirms its readable number. | An unresolved read needs exception handling. |
| Better mechanism | The controller associates the bag with its flight and tracks its position. | Flight data and physical position must stay linked. |
| Test | A tan bag for Flight 17 passes the junction; the diverter then sends the blue bag onto the Flight 24 belt. | The device acts for the correct individual bag, at the correct moment. |
| Payoff | Blue suitcase rests at FLIGHT 24's collection point. | Identification plus control determines its path. |

## Factual and illustrative boundaries

The verified claims and primary sources are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The story depicts one supported approach, not a universal layout for every airport. Other systems may use RFID, tilt trays or individual carriers.

The hero bag enters the depicted sorting zone **after screening**. The first view is the belt disappearing behind a terminal wall, not a complete check-in-to-aircraft journey. A tiny set sign, `FROM SCREENING`, can establish the inlet; it is not a narrated extra step.

The operator recovery is an illustrative successful case: the barcode is obscured while its human-readable number remains available in a captured image. The operator confirms that number against an existing record; the computer does not infer a destination from the bag's color. The check is compressed into the story's short runtime and makes no claim about actual handling time. The white tag remains the same tag throughout.

The pusher pauses the local main conveyor during its short transfer in this simplified scene. The scene dimensions and actuation timings in the production directions are staging choices, not airport engineering specifications. Flight allocation is held fixed for this one demonstration. Reconciliation and loading checks are subsequent processes and are not replaced by reaching the collection belt.
