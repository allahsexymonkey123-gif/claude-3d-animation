## Overlay rules for this plan

The caption sequences above are editorial chunks, not full subtitles with verified word timings. Keep each chunk stable, white with a heavy charcoal outline. Use at most two short lines, no bouncing text. Fit their actual windows to the checked voice.

Use a higher caption band centered around 64% of picture height in the wide and plan views, and around 73% in scanner/section inserts. Reserve the lower 15% for platform UI. Reposition captions to protect the tag, transfer contact and destination sign; keep the chosen position stable within each beat. Use only one part label at a time. The operator panel and flight record are short diagrams attached to the workstation area, not paragraphs covering the world.

The opening hook belongs near 10% of frame height. Remove it by f90 before part labels begin. Failure status, operator panel and speech captions must not overlap. Labels use world-projected attachment points; hide a leader when its target is occluded.
